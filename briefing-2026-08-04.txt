Metabolic Vulnerability, or MVX, is a measure of metabolic frailty, which connotes susceptibility to dying. The episode discusses how MVX is a separate influence on mortality, distinct from the risk of developing a particular disease. The MVX assay is a composite score that measures six things, including small HDLP, glyc A, citrate, and the three branched chain amino acids. As the episode explains, and I quote, "The score seems to have remarkable predictive value of all sorts of things we want to avoid, starting with death, cardiovascular death, and liver disease." This is particularly relevant for engineering managers, as it can help them understand the importance of prioritizing their own health and wellness, as well as that of their team members.
